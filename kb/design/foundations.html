Radius & elevation

Radius is fixed per component family; there are two shadow recipes plus the raised-button glow.

Control sizes

Two sets coexist: the LESS btn-height-* tokens and the Ant controlHeight overrides. Inputs, selects and date pickers share a height scale.

Iconography

The <Icon icon="MdEdit" /> atom resolves a string name against four sources in this order : components/icons.js (react-icons re-exports) → components/svg.js (local product artwork: brand marks, empty-state illustrations, BayutMatchIcon) → widgets/AmenitiesSvg → widgets/NewAmenitiesSvg. Passing styled wraps the glyph in the icon container tile. Country flags come from country-flag-icons (3×2).

Product artwork is not drawn in components: illustrations come from svg.js exports (EmptyListing, EmptyListingWidget, NetworkError, NoRecord) and from public/profolio-assets/ (188 files — per-tenant favicons and logos, images/maintenance.svg, ph-listings.svg, limit-credits.svg, REGA / ministry badges, lite/banner-faces.png). Use those files rather than redrawing.
