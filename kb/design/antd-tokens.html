antd resolved tokens

Everything the product leaves to antd. These are not in its source because it never overrides them: antd v5 computes them at runtime from the seed config in src/theme/index.js. Running that algorithm here is how they stop being guesses.

Installed antd 5.22.1; the app pins ^5.20.0.

Full output: data/antd-tokens.json (502 tokens).

These are antd's global tokens. Per-component overrides in the product's components: block (Card.paddingLG, Button.controlHeight and so on) are the app's own and are already in foundations.html; they are not re-derived here.
