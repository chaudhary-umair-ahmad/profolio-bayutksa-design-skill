Platform brand colour

platformList[].brandColor = tenantTheme['primary-color']

This is the accent behind the chart line, the credit meter and the tab ink bar. For bayut it is not API-supplied — it resolves to the primary colour. (zameen hardcodes its own per platform; bayut does not.)
