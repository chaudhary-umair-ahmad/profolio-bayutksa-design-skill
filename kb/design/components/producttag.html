Product tag

  • source product-tag/index.js
  • status n/a

common/product-tag — a Tag specialisation for paid products: 10px/700, white text, no border, 6px padding, line-height 1, soft-light gradient over the product colour, radius 4px, optional leading icon container.

Real defaults (product-tag/index.js): shape defaults to "round", the background comes from iconProps.color (falling back to tagBackgroundColor, then gray100), the leading icon is 1em inside a 1.4em container in white, and tagSpace is 6px — or 4px 6px when hideIconInTag is set. The name is translated through i18n.
