Spinner, skeleton & progress

  • layer atom
  • source —

common/spinner uses Ant's LoadingOutlined indicator; type="full" centres it in a full-viewport .spin layer over rgba(255,255,255,.05). Skeletons take their gradient from border-color-light. Progress keeps rgba(0,0,0,.06) as the remaining track; the standalone ProgressBar is 300 × 20px with a centred percentage.
