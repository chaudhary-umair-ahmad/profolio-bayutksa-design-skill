common/custom-card — an icon or avatar, a 600-weight title (14px desktop / 12px mobile) and a 12px gray description, with optional extraContent pushed to the end and a 20px gap. It becomes a link (internal Link or external anchor) when link is set, and verified rows tint the title primary-color at 67% alpha.
