Card frame

common/cards — the wrapper every widget, table and section sits in. Head is at least 47px with an 18px h4 title; body padding 12px; radius 8px (10px card token) with a 1px border on desktop that drops to 0 below 992px. Card-level tabs get a 24px gutter and an ink bar in --_accent-color, which defaults to primary-color and can be overridden per card.

Global card rules (style.less): head inline padding is 24px (20px below 768px, line-height 1 below 576px), the shadow is 0 5px 20px #9299b803, the head extra slot is a centred flex row whose links are #868eae with the overflow dropdown ordered last at a 20px inline-start margin, and below 576px the head stacks into a column. A card-body spinner is absolutely centred with a 200px minimum height. The card nav (.card-nav) is 12px / 500 in #868eae, active in primary with a 1px underline 19px below.
