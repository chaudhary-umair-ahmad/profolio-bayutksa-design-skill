Which fonts actually load

Read from:

  • src/hooks/useAppInit.js
  • public/profolio-assets/bayut/fonts/

src/theme/index.js names two stacks: theme['font-family'] is Lato and the antd v5 token fontFamily is Figtree. Only one of them is ever fetched.

useAppInit.js injects these at runtime:

  • /profolio-assets/bayut/fonts/arabic-font.css
  • /profolio-assets/bayut/fonts/currency-font.css
  • /profolio-assets/bayut/fonts/lato-font.css

Nothing anywhere loads Figtree. Lato is what paints.

The faces that ship

familyweightfile
Lato300Lato-Light.woff2
Lato400Lato-Regular.woff2
Lato700Lato-Bold.woff2

Three weights, not nine. .fw-500 and .fw-600 are everywhere in the product and neither has a real face: the browser synthesises them from Regular. A design that asks for a true 500 or 600 will look heavier than the live screen.

The currency mark

currency-font.css is an icomoon glyph font, keyed on [class^="currency-"]. ر.س is an icon, not Arabic text — which is why it sits left of the number in an LTR row without any bidi handling.
